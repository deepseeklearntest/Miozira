# Prototype 0.1 family-test handoff

Status: TEMPLATE — complete only after the exact release record is signed off.

The adult tester should use at most one calm, optional session per day. Silence, replay, stopping, and mispronunciation are normal; the product is being tested, not the child.

Before starting, confirm airplane mode, comfortable volume, the parent gate, optional microphone consent, family-voice flow, transfer marking, reset explanation, and the stop conditions in `docs/05-quality/family-test-protocol.md`.

If a privacy, safety, distress, data-loss, or canonical-audio failure occurs: stop the test, preserve the build record, and contact the project owner. Do not update the app or tune thresholds during the seven-day run.
